# MSE-booklet
Students are welcome to suggest resources related to the course, including:

images;
videos;
news articles;
practical applications;
other relevant educational materials.

To submit a suggestion, open a new issue and complete the requested fields.

Please provide the original source of the resource and briefly explain its relevance to the course. Submitted materials will be reviewed before being included on the website.

Corrections and feedback

Errors, unclear passages, and other comments concerning the booklet may also be reported through the Issues section.
